About me

My name is Rafael Rossato. I am a member of Group 10 in the IFPR Integrated Project, and I live in São José das Palmeiras, Paraná.

In this project, I am learning how financial and tax education can help people plan their spending, avoid waste, and make more responsible decisions.

My education

I am a student at the Federal Institute of Paraná (IFPR). I have been building my educational journey through school experiences, spending time with classmates and teachers, and taking part in educational projects.

An important experience in my journey

Taking part in an Integrated Project has given me the opportunity to develop my knowledge and better understand the relationship between education, economics, and society.

Where I live

Centro neighborhood — São José das Palmeiras — Paraná

São José das Palmeiras is located in western Paraná. According to Brazil's 2022 Census by IBGE, the municipality has 3,957 residents.

Its economy is connected to livestock farming, agriculture, commerce, and services. Financial planning is therefore important for families, workers, rural producers, and small business owners.

What I am doing now and my future plans

I currently study at IFPR and take part in an Integrated Project about financial and tax education. I am learning to connect what I study at school with real-life situations.

I plan to continue studying and gaining knowledge that can help me manage my personal life and contribute to the community where I live.
